Drafting the answer

Keep the answer short enough to file fast and specific enough to preserve the defenses that matter.

S1 What changed
Law changed Use current motion language

Defective service can support more than a continuance.

Preserve dismissal, vacatur, and sealing language when the file shows bad service or an old default based on bad service.

S2 Drafting checklist
  • 1Deny what you cannot verify. Do not admit dates, balances, or service facts without documents.
  • 2Preserve notice defects. Name the notice type and the missing requirement.
  • 3Preserve service defects. Say who served it, what method was used, and why it fails.
  • 4Ask for the remedy. Dismissal, time to respond, sealing, or another specific order.
S3 Copy block
Sample language

Tenant denies that Plaintiff properly served the summons and complaint. Tenant preserves all defenses based on defective service, insufficient notice, improper calculation of rent, retaliation, discrimination, and breach of the warranty of habitability.
