When the clock starts

The first defense move is not the argument. It is finding the date that makes every later deadline honest.

0% through this course
S1 The scenario

A client brings in a notice to quit, a summons, and a screenshot from the landlord. Three dates appear in three different places. Only one of them starts the court timeline.

S2 The rule
  • Notice Receipt dateThe notice period usually runs from when the tenant receives the notice, not when the landlord prints it.
  • Summons Service dateThe answer date depends on proper service of the summons and complaint.
  • Court Entry dateCheck the docket because a late or early entry can create a procedural defense.
S3 Try it
Quick read

The landlord dated a 14-day notice on May 1, mailed it on May 2, and the tenant received it on May 6. Which date do you use first?

The answer: start with actual receipt, then test whether the later summons and court entry respected the full notice period.
