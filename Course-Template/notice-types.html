The four notice types

A notice tells you what the landlord thinks the case is. Your job is to decide whether the document can carry that theory.

0% through this course
S1 The map
Nonpayment

14-day

Look for the cure language, amount claimed, and whether fees are disguised as rent.

No fault

30-day

Check the rental period and whether the tenancy type supports the timeline.

Cause

Lease breach

Demand detail. Vague conduct language can fail before the merits.

S2 The rule

The notice must give the tenant enough information to understand what is being alleged, what deadline applies, and what can be done before the landlord files a summary process case.

Practice move
When the notice theory and the complaint theory do not match, flag it immediately. That mismatch can narrow the hearing or support dismissal.
S3 Remember
  • Type first, facts second. The notice type determines the proof path.
  • Nonpayment notices deserve a rent-ledger audit before any answer is drafted.
  • Cause notices must say enough for the tenant to respond.
