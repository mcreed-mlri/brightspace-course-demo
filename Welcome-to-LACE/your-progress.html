Your progress & completion

You don't have to mark anything done, click a "complete" button, or submit a report. LACE tracks your progress quietly as you go. Here's exactly what counts, and where it shows up.

0% through this course
§ 1 The moment

A required course can make you anxious about the bookkeeping: Did that count? Do I have to do something to log it? Will my supervisor see I finished?

Good news — the answer to all of it is "it's handled." Look just under this topic's title: that little bar has been filling as you move through the course. That's your progress, tracking itself.

§ 2 How completion works

Completion in LACE is based on one simple thing: visiting a topic marks it complete. Open it, and it counts. That's the whole rule.

Where your progress shows up
  1. The progress bar on each topic — the thin line under the title, filling as you go.
  2. The course outline (your Home page) — finished topics flip to Done, and the Continue ring shows your percent.
  3. The completion screen — finish the last topic and you land on a "you're done" page with your totals.
  4. The Hub — your overall percent for the course is reported back, so a required course reads as 100% once you've finished.

Because it's visit-based, you can leave anytime and your place is saved. The Continue card on your Home page always points to the next thing you haven't done. And if you ever want a clean slate, the Home page has a small "↻ Reset course progress" link at the bottom.

§ 3 Try it

One question. You'll see the answer once you choose.

Quick check

You open a topic, read the first section, and close the tab to take a call. Does that topic count as complete?

The answer: visiting the topic is enough — it's marked complete and your spot is saved. There's no button to press and no report to file. (Of course, it's worth actually reading it!)
§ 4 If you remember nothing else
  • Visiting a topic completes it — no button, no submission.
  • Your Home outline shows what's done and what's next.
  • Your percent reports back to the Hub, so required courses read as finished.
