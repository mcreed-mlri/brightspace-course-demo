Track progress and completion

You should not have to wonder whether a required course counted. The Hub shows your progress, and Brightspace keeps the official completion record.

§ 1 What counts

In this course setup, opening a topic marks that topic complete. There is no separate "mark complete" button to find, and Try-it checks are not scored.

The progress bar under the topic title fills as you move through the course. On the course Home page, completed topics show as Done and the Continue card points to the next unfinished topic.

§ 2 Where the record lives
Progress in two places
  1. Inside the course: the wrapper remembers local progress so the outline and Continue card feel useful.
  2. In Brightspace: Brightspace records the official visited-topic activity and completion data.
  3. In the Hub: the Hub can show progress and resume links so you do not have to dig for them.

If a supervisor, program, or completion report needs a training record, Brightspace is the source underneath. The Hub is the easier way to see and use that information.

§ 3 Try it

One question. Choose an answer to reveal the explanation.

Quick check

You open a topic, then close the tab because you have to take a call. What happens to your course progress?

The answer: the topic counts as visited. The Hub and course outline are designed to help you resume without bookkeeping.
§ 4 If you remember nothing else
  • Opening a topic counts in this course setup.
  • Brightspace is the official completion record underneath.
  • Continue helps you resume from the next unfinished topic.
