Use Try-it checks

Try-it checks are short practice moments. They are there to help the idea stick, not to grade you or slow you down.

0% through this course
§ 1 Why they exist

A Try-it asks you to pause for a few seconds and choose an answer. That tiny act of recall is what turns "I read the page" into "I can use the idea later."

You will usually see one near the end of a topic. Choose an answer, read the explanation, and keep moving.

§ 2 Ground rules
What to expect
  1. Not graded: no score is saved and no wrong answer counts against you.
  2. Immediate explanation: the answer appears after you choose.
  3. Best guesses are fine: choosing helps you remember, even when you miss.
  4. Short by design: one focused check, not a test bank.
§ 3 Try it

One question. Choose an answer to reveal the explanation.

Quick check

You are not sure which Try-it answer is right. What should you do?

The answer: make your best guess. Try-it checks are low-stakes practice, and the explanation is the point.
§ 4 If you remember nothing else
  • Try-it checks are practice, not grades.
  • The explanation appears after you choose.
  • A best guess is useful because recall is the learning.
