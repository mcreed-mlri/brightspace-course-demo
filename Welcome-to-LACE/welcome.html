§ 1 The moment

It's 2:40 on a Tuesday. You have a client at 3:00 and a question you've never had to answer before. You don't have an afternoon to read a manual — you have the twenty minutes between meetings.

That moment is what LACE is built for. Not a binder you read once and shelve, but short, findable answers you can reach for when the work is actually in front of you.

§ 2 How LACE works

LACE — MLRI's Legal Aid and Community Education hub — is your home for training. Everything is organized into courses, and every course is broken into small topics you can finish in a few minutes each.

The shape of the hub
  1. The Hub — your front door. It shows the courses assigned to you and how far along you are.
  2. A course — a handful of related topics, like the five in this one.
  3. A topic — one focused idea, 1–3 minutes, ending in a quick question to make it stick.

Small pieces are deliberate. You can finish a topic in a coffee break, come back later, and pick up exactly where you left off — the hub remembers for you.

§ 3 Try it

One question. You'll see the answer once you choose.

Quick check

You have about ten minutes free before your next call. What's the LACE-sized thing to do with it?

The answer: topics are small on purpose. A few minutes is enough to finish one and have it count — you never have to clear your whole calendar to make real progress.
§ 4 If you remember nothing else
  • LACE is your learning hub — courses made of small topics.
  • Topics are short on purpose, built for real moments between work.
  • You can stop and resume anytime — the hub remembers where you are.
