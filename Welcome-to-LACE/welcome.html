Start here: what the Hub is

The Learning Hub is your front door for training. This short course shows you what to do first, how to move through assigned courses, and how to know that your work counted.

§ 1 What it is

Brightspace is the system that hosts courses and records completion. The Learning Hub is the cleaner front door we use around it: the place where you find assigned learning, resume what you started, and see what needs your attention.

You do not need to learn every Brightspace menu. Start from the Hub, open the course that is assigned to you, and use the course controls at the top of each page.

§ 2 Your first three moves
When you first arrive
  1. Check your assigned learning. Start with anything marked required, new, or in progress.
  2. Use Continue. The Continue card points to the next topic you should open.
  3. Do one small piece. Courses are broken into short topics so you can make progress between real work.

This course is the model. The buttons, outline, progress bar, and quick checks you see here are the same patterns you will see in other Hub courses.

§ 3 Try it

One question. Choose an answer to reveal the explanation.

Quick check

You sign in and see several courses. What is the best first move?

The answer: start with assigned or required learning. The Hub is there to reduce hunting, and Continue keeps you moving from the right spot.
§ 4 If you remember nothing else
  • The Learning Hub is your front door for training.
  • Brightspace still keeps the official course record underneath.
  • Start with assigned learning, then use Continue.
