Find your assigned learning

You should not have to guess where to click. The Hub and course wrapper are built around a few repeated controls: Continue, the outline, the menu, and the Read/Practice toggle.

§ 1 The main path

The simplest path is: open the Hub, choose assigned learning, and follow Continue. On a course Home page, Continue points to the first unfinished topic. Inside a topic, Next moves you forward and Previous moves you back.

What each control is for
  1. Continue card: resume the next topic you have not finished.
  2. Course outline: see every topic, time estimate, and completion status.
  3. Menu button: jump to another topic without clicking Previous over and over.
  4. Exit to Hub: leave the course and return to the larger Hub dashboard.
§ 2 Read vs Practice

The Read / Practice toggle changes how a topic is paced. It does not change the requirements, your completion, or the content you receive. It only changes the way the page is presented.

Use the mode that fits the moment
  1. Read: shows the full topic on one page. Use it when you want to skim, review, search, or move quickly.
  2. Practice: shows one section at a time. Use it when you want fewer distractions or want the idea to stick.
  3. Switch anytime: your progress is not reset. The toggle is just a reading preference.

If you are in a rush, Read is probably right. If you are learning something new or easy to mix up, Practice gives you a little more focus.

Quick check

You need to re-open topic one, and you are currently on topic four. What is the fastest course control to use?

The answer: use the menu for jumps. Read and Practice change the pace of the page, not where you are in the course.
§ 4 If you remember nothing else
  • Continue resumes your next unfinished topic.
  • The menu is for jumping; Next and Previous are for stepping.
  • Read is full-page review; Practice is one section at a time.
