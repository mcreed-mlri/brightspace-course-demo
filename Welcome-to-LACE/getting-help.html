§ 1 When something's off

Eventually a page won't load, a link will look wrong, or you'll just want a course on something you don't see yet. None of that is on you to diagnose — there's a person for it.

For anything technical or any "should there be a course on…?" question, reach the LACE team at support@example.org. Tell us the course and topic you were on and we'll take it from there.

§ 2 Staying current & what's next

LACE isn't static — courses get updated as practice and the law change. When that happens, the hub flags it for you so you don't have to wonder what's fresh. Watch for these small pills on the outline:

● New ● Updated ● Law changed

A New pill means a topic was just added; Updated means the content was refreshed; Law changed means something legal moved and you should look again even if you've finished it before.

From here: head back to the Hub to see the rest of the courses assigned to you. You'll find them all in the same shape you just learned — so you already know your way around.

§ 3 Try it

Quick check

A course you finished months ago now shows a ● Law changed pill. What does that mean for you?

The answer: a "Law changed" pill is the hub telling you the ground shifted. Your progress is safe, but it's worth re-opening the updated topic so what you know stays accurate.
