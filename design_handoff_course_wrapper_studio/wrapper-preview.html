The seam between hub & Brightspace

The course, wearing the hub's clothes.

Brightspace hosts the content — but its heavy header and fixed left menu starve the page and look nothing like your A2 hub. This wrapper restyles the whole course into the Studio language (Geist, cool grays, brand blue, hairlines) and replaces Brightspace's chrome with a slim bar or the hub's own rail. Same surfaces, same type, same blue. No whiplash.

The handoff moment

Clicking a course never breaks the contract.

From the hub you tap Resume. A quiet bridge holds the same background and the course title while Brightspace boots — no white flash, no LMS shell — then the outline arrives in the same skin. Press play.

Deploy

3 steps
  1. Swap course-style.css and course-nav.js in your course package. No HTML edits needed.
  2. Pick a treatment: set chromeMode to "bar" or "rail" in course-config.js.
  3. Flip deployMode back to "lms" and re-upload. The Brightspace URLs in config take over.
