# Legal Aid LMS Content Creation Template

Use this template to draft training content for the Legal Aid LMS in Brightspace/LACE. Write in plain language. You do not need to know HTML, CSS, Brightspace file paths, or any technical setup details.

When you want a designed element, mark it with brackets such as `[CALLOUT]`, `[ACCORDION]`, or `[TABS]`. The MLRI/LACE builder will turn those notes into the right Brightspace page design.

This template is for legal aid training modules. The LMS may track completion, participation, certificates, assessments, reflections, or required practice activities.

## Design Component Menu

Use these bracketed labels anywhere in your draft. The builder will convert them into designed Brightspace/LACE components.

### Callout

Use `[CALLOUT]` for key rules, warnings, practice tips, deadlines, or "do not miss this" ideas.

Example:

`[CALLOUT: Practice tip - Always confirm whether the deadline is counted in calendar days or court days before advising a client.]`

Optional tone labels:

- `[CALLOUT WARNING: ...]`
- `[CALLOUT TIP: ...]`
- `[CALLOUT KEY RULE: ...]`
- `[CALLOUT DEADLINE: ...]`

### Accordion

Use `[ACCORDION]` for FAQs, optional deeper explanations, common misconceptions, or details that learners should be able to expand only when needed.

Example:

```text
[ACCORDION: What if the client missed the answer deadline?]
Explain the options, risks, and next steps here.
[/ACCORDION]
```

### Tabs

Use `[TABS]` when learners need to compare categories, jurisdictions, forms, procedural stages, or strategic choices.

Example:

```text
[TABS: Compare notice types]
[TAB: 14-day notice]
Use this tab for nonpayment cases.
[TAB: 30-day notice]
Use this tab for no-fault or cause-based termination, depending on the facts.
[/TABS]
```

### Quote

Use `[QUOTE]` for statutes, cases, model language, policy excerpts, client narratives, or expert commentary.

Example:

`[QUOTE: "The purpose of the interview is not only to collect facts, but to identify the client's goal."]`

### Checklist

Use `[CHECKLIST]` for repeatable legal tasks, filing steps, intake steps, review processes, or supervisor check-ins.

Example:

```text
[CHECKLIST: Before filing]
- Confirm the deadline.
- Review the required form.
- Check whether service is complete.
- Save a copy for the client file.
[/CHECKLIST]
```

### Scenario

Use `[SCENARIO]` for hypotheticals, client stories, issue spotting, or practice-based learning.

Example:

```text
[SCENARIO: Client intake]
Maria received a notice from her landlord and is worried she has to leave immediately. She brings the notice, a rent ledger, and text messages from the property manager.

Question: What are the first facts the advocate should identify?
[/SCENARIO]
```

### Knowledge Check

Use `[KNOWLEDGE CHECK]` for short, low-stakes questions that help learners confirm understanding.

Example:

```text
[KNOWLEDGE CHECK]
Question: Which document usually tells you the alleged reason for termination?
Choices:
A. Lease
B. Notice to quit
C. Rent receipt
Correct answer: B
Feedback: The notice usually frames the reason and timeline for the next step.
[/KNOWLEDGE CHECK]
```

### Assessment

Use `[ASSESSMENT]` when the learner's answer should count for completion, certification, supervisor review, or a required training record.

Example:

```text
[ASSESSMENT: Required completion question]
Question: List two steps an advocate should take before advising a client about a filing deadline.
Response type: Short answer
Passing/completion rule: Learner must submit a response.
Reviewer, if any: Training team
[/ASSESSMENT]
```

### Download

Use `[DOWNLOAD]` for PDFs, forms, templates, sample pleadings, handouts, or offline checklists.

Example:

`[DOWNLOAD: Sample answer form - Attach the latest approved PDF or link.]`

### Internal Conversion Notes

- Convert bracket tags into LACE components from `Interactive-Elements-Demo`.
- Build each trackable page from `Course-Template/topic-template.html`.
- Keep filenames short, lowercase, and descriptive.
- Preserve citations and source notes even when they are not displayed directly to learners.
- If completion matters, create a Brightspace Content topic and use the Brightspace topic URL in `course-config.js`.
- If an assessment matters, confirm whether it should be a lightweight knowledge check, a Brightspace quiz, a reflection submission, or another tracked activity.
